# KindWorks.AI — Brand & Design Context

Source: KindWorks.AI Design Guidelines, Live — Jan 2025.

## Colors

### Primary
- Coral `#FF474A` (shade `#FF8484`) — bold statements, passion
- Green `#2E6661` (shade `#1B4239`) — foundation, intelligence, data
- Yellow `#FFB500` (shade `#FFD166`) — positivity, warmth (accent)

### Secondary
- Purple `#9C338C` (shade `#6F0E60`) — inspiration
- Mint `#8FE0BF` (shade `#4DAE97`) — soft data visuals
- White `#FFFFFF`, Near-Black `#1E1E1E`

### Semantic meaning
- Dark green → hard data / proof points
- Mint → cited research (soft data)
- Purple → useful content, inspiration
- Yellow → celebrations, warmth
- Coral → big statements, alerts

## Typography
- **Headings:** Jeko Bold (fallback: Inter)
- **Serif emphasis:** Tiempos Headline Light (fallback: Source Serif 4 / Fraunces)
- **Body:** DM Sans Regular

## Layout
- Generous whitespace
- Rounded corners everywhere (cards 20–32px, buttons pill)
- Grid background on dark-green hero sections (signature look)
- "The Dot" — small accent circles pulled from the `.AI`

## Tone
Warm, plain, optimistic. Short sentences. Second person. Never corporate.
